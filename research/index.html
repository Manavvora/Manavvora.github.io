Publications & preprints

Scalable multi-agent learning for autonomous systems.

I develop learning, planning, and control methods for autonomous systems operating under resource, information, communication, and sensing constraints.

Research threads

Scaling autonomy where coordination is constrained.

    Scalable Multi-Agent Learning

    Communication and coordination methods that remain effective as agent populations grow.

    SCoUT · scalable communication and coordination

    CurrentScalable sparse multi-agent diffusion

  2. 02

    Resource-Aware Decision-Making

    Planning and learning under limited budgets, capacity, and partial observability.

    IEEE Control Systems Letters · TMLR · IEEE RA-L

  3. 03

    Information-Efficient Robotics

    Robot control and planning that activate only the information and sensing needed for the task.

    ComTraQ-MPC · B-COD / just-enough sensing

    CurrentHeterogeneous-sensor multi-robot search and rescue

  4. 04

    Strategic Coordination & Prediction

    Planning and prediction when agents must resolve conflicts and act through uncertain occlusions.

    Predicted Reachability · strategic planning and control
